Help & support.

Find the basics for checking in and understanding your attendance record.

Using your QR pass

Open the pass before you reach the attendance desk. Keep the full code visible so it can be scanned clearly.

Location information

The application may record GPS coordinates at check-in. The production version should show permission, accuracy and error states clearly.

A missing arrival?

Check your history first. If a record is missing or its location needs review, contact the administrator for your branch.

Frequently asked questions

Does the QR code mean I am already checked in?

No. This demonstration shows the pass only. In your connected workflow, an authorised check-in action needs to create the arrival record in the backend.

Why does my location need to be recorded?

Your current repository includes location coordinates in the QR/check-in flow. The live app should communicate when they are captured and how location errors are handled.

Can I edit an arrival record?

This prototype does not edit backend records. A production workflow should route corrections to authorised staff and preserve an audit trail.
